### 4.2 Official Competition Rules
1. **No Flag / Hint Sharing:** Teams must work independently. Sharing flags, solutions, hints, or exploit scripts between different teams before the competition ends will result in immediate disqualification of all involved teams.
2. **No Infrastructure Attacks:** Attacking the CTF scoreboard (`CTFd`), non-challenge infrastructure, other participants, or performing automated brute-force/DDoS/directory fuzzing (unless explicitly stated in a challenge description) is strictly prohibited.
3. **Rate Limiting & Flag Brute-Forcing:** Automated flag submission brute-forcing is prohibited and blocked via rate limiting.
4. **Mandatory Write-Up Verification:** Top-ranking teams eligible for prizes must submit detailed technical write-ups and solver scripts within `TBD (e.g., 12 / 24 hours)` of competition close to verify organic solves.
5. **Tie-Breaking Rule:** In the event of identical point totals, the team that reached that score **first** (earliest timestamp of the last valid flag submission) ranks higher, with zero time penalty for incorrect submissions (`confirm if any penalty applies`).
