## 5. Decisions Made and Why
- **Decision:** Number top-level folders `00` through `10` chronologically matching the event lifecycle.
- **Rationale:** Ensures automatic chronological ordering in file explorers and automated Pandoc document concatenation.

## 6. Challenges Faced and How They Were Solved
- **Challenge:** Maintaining broken-link-free navigation across 50+ interlinked Markdown files.
- **Solution:** Standardized relative paths and included automated link-checking in `scripts/build-docs.ps1` and `scripts/build-docs.sh`.
