## 3. Volunteer Briefing Rules (Read Aloud at T-1 Orientation)
1. **Punctuality:** Report to the Helpdesk **15 minutes before** your shift starts for briefing and badge pickup.
2. **Never Leave a Station Unattended:** Do not leave your assigned zone until your relief volunteer for the next shift has physically arrived and signed in.
3. **Escalation Rule:** Volunteers do **not** make rulings on CTF challenges or competition rules. Direct all challenge questions to the Discord `#support-tickets` system or summon a Development Team member.
4. **Safety First:** Keep all walkways clear of cables, monitor participant well-being, and immediately notify the Shift Supervisor of any medical or electrical issue.
