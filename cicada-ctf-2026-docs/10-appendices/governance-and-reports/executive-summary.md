## 1. Event Overview & Institutional Context
**Cicada CTF 2026** is the flagship Capture The Flag cybersecurity competition jointly organized by **Birla Institute of Technology (BIT), Mesra** and the **IEEE Student Branch of BIT Mesra**, scheduled for **early December 2026**. The competition challenges student teams to solve realistic offensive and defensive security problems across seven technical categories: **Web Exploitation, Cryptography, Binary Exploitation (Pwn), Reverse Engineering, Digital Forensics, Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT), and Miscellaneous**.
