## 4. Detailed Description
### 4.1 Multi-Channel Outreach Strategy
1. **BIT Mesra Internal Engagement:** Classroom announcements, hostel notice boards, official student mailing lists, and beginner CTF orientation sessions (`confirm if workshop/orientation is held`).
2. **Inter-College & University Club Network:** Direct invitations sent to Faculty Advisors, Technical Secretaries, and Cybersecurity Club Leads across technical universities.
3. **IEEE Network Leverage:** Cross-promotion through neighboring IEEE Student Branches and Section newsletters.
4. **Competitive CTF Community Listing:** Listing on competition aggregators (`TBD: CTFtime / Unstop / Devfolio`).
