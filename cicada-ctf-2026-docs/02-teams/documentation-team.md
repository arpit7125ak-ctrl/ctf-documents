# Documentation Team

## 1. Overview
The **Documentation Team** serves as the institutional memory and official chronicler of **Cicada CTF 2026**. Led by **Gaurav Gupta**, the team is responsible for designing, maintaining, verifying, and publishing the end-to-end event record, meeting minutes, operational templates, internal directories, and post-event reports for BIT Mesra, the IEEE Student Branch, and external sponsors.

---

## Team Mission and Responsibilities
- **Mission:** Deliver an accurate, zero-fabrication, well-structured documentation package covering the entire lifecycle of Cicada CTF 2026 from initial ideation through post-event wrap-up.
- **Core Responsibilities:**
  1. Record and distribute meeting minutes within 24 hours of every general and inter-team meeting.
  2. Maintain the [Missing Information Tracker](../10-appendices/missing-information-tracker.md) and follow up with team leads weekly.
  3. Collect, verify, and format inputs from Logistics, PR & Design, Sponsorship, Outreach, and Development teams.
  4. Coordinate with photographers/media volunteers to catalog event photos in [photo-index.md](../09-media/photo-index.md).
  5. Compile the Executive Summary, Sponsor/College Report, and final PDF/DOCX/website deliverables.

## Workflow and Tools Used
- **Authoring Format:** GitHub-Flavored Markdown (`.md`) with Mermaid diagrams.
- **Version Control & Collaboration:** Git repository (`/cicada-ctf-2026-docs`), GitHub repo (`arpit7125ak-ctrl/ctf-documents`), Google Forms for intake questionnaires.
- **Build & Export Stack:** Pandoc (PDF/DOCX generation), MkDocs (static documentation portal), PowerShell (`build-docs.ps1`), Bash (`build-docs.sh`).
- **Review Cadence:** Weekly sync with all counterpart team leads; 3-stage verification (`Draft` → `Team Lead Review` → `Core Approval`).
