# Outreach Team

## 1. Overview
The **Outreach Team** leads participant acquisition, inter-college promotion, cybersecurity community partnerships, and participant communication for **Cicada CTF 2026**. Led by **Saurav Pradhan**, the team ensures strong national and regional participation from university CTF teams, IEEE student branches, and cybersecurity clubs, while serving as the primary communication bridge for registered participants.

---

## Team Mission and Responsibilities
- **Mission:** Drive high-quality registrations from diverse institutions and maintain clear, timely, and helpful communication with all participants throughout the competition lifecycle.
- **Core Responsibilities:**
  1. Build a database of technical universities, NITs, IITs, IIITs, IEEE student branches, and college cybersecurity/coding clubs.
  2. Execute multi-channel outreach campaigns (official emails, campus ambassadors, CTFtime listing `if applicable`, Discord/WhatsApp communities).
  3. Manage the participant registration funnel, eligibility verification, and team formation compliance.
  4. Draft and send all participant emails, instructions, rulebooks, and Discord announcements.
  5. Operate the participant query helpdesk before and during the event.
