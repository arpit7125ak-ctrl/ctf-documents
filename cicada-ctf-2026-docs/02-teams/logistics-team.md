# Logistics Team

## 1. Overview
The **Logistics Team** is responsible for all physical, operational, scheduling, hospitality, and on-ground execution aspects of **Cicada CTF 2026**. Led by **Shounak Ray**, the team oversees venue preparation, seating arrangements, power distribution, local networking, schedule adherence, volunteer management, hospitality, and emergency safety readiness at BIT Mesra.

---

## Team Mission and Responsibilities
- **Mission:** Provide a reliable, safe, and friction-free operational environment for participants, guests, volunteers, and organizers throughout Cicada CTF 2026.
- **Core Responsibilities:**
  1. Secure BIT Mesra administrative approvals, hall bookings, and security clearances.
  2. Plan and execute venue setup, seating layouts, power distribution, and physical networking (`if Offline/Hybrid`).
  3. Author and enforce the master [Schedule and Run of Show](../06-logistics/schedule-and-run-of-show.md).
  4. Recruit, train, and manage student volunteers using the [Volunteer Duty Roster](../10-appendices/templates/volunteer-duty-roster.md).
  5. Coordinate catering, participant travel assistance, guest hospitality, and emergency medical/safety protocols.
