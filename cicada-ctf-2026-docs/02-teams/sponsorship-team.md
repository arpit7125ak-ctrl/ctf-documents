# Sponsorship Team

## 1. Overview
The **Sponsorship Team** drives the financial sustainability and corporate partnership ecosystem of **Cicada CTF 2026**. Led by **Anooz Burnwal**, the team identifies prospective cybersecurity firms, cloud providers, and institutional partners, pitches partnership tiers, negotiates Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs), tracks sponsor deliverables, and secures prize pool funding and in-kind resources (such as cloud credits and licenses).

---

## Team Mission and Responsibilities
- **Mission:** Secure financial and in-kind sponsorships that fund a competitive prize pool, robust cloud infrastructure, and high-quality participant experience while delivering measurable brand value to partners.
- **Core Responsibilities:**
  1. Define sponsorship tiers (Title, Platinum, Gold, Silver, In-Kind/Infrastructure, Community) and benefits.
  2. Build and maintain a targeted CRM pipeline of cybersecurity firms, cloud providers, tech companies, and alumni-led enterprises.
  3. Conduct outreach, pitch calls, and MoU negotiations.
  4. Track and verify every promised sponsor deliverable using the [Sponsor Deliverable Tracker](../10-appendices/templates/sponsor-deliverable-tracker.md).
  5. Coordinate invoice processing, fund receipts, and post-event sponsor ROI reports.
