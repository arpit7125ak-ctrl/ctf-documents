# PR & Design Team

## 1. Overview
The **PR & Design Team** crafts the visual identity, branding, multimedia assets, public relations messaging, and social media presence of **Cicada CTF 2026**. The team ensures cohesive visual storytelling across digital campaigns, sponsorship collateral, platform UI themes, and physical venue branding.

---

## Team Mission and Responsibilities
- **Mission:** Establish a distinctive, high-impact cybersecurity brand identity for Cicada CTF 2026 and maximize engagement across social and campus channels.
- **Core Responsibilities:**
  1. Design the official Cicada CTF 2026 logo, color palette, typography, and brand kit.
  2. Create digital assets (announcement posters, countdown graphics, speaker/sponsor spotlights, reels/teaser videos).
  3. Design print collateral (stage backdrops, standees, ID cards, certificates, stickers, t-shirts/swag).
  4. Manage social media content calendars, copywriting, and live event coverage across Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Discord.
  5. Lead event photography and videography in coordination with the Documentation Team.
