### 4.2 Shift Rotation Policy
For overnight or 24+ hour competition formats, volunteers are scheduled in **6-to-8-hour staggered shifts** (`Shift A: Morning`, `Shift B: Evening`, `Shift C: Overnight`) so no volunteer is overworked and every station maintains 100% coverage.
