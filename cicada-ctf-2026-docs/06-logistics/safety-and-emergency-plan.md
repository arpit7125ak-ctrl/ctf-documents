## 4. Detailed Description
### 4.1 Emergency Response Matrix
*(Note: Direct personal phone numbers of student organizers are stored in `10-appendices/contact-directory-INTERNAL.md`; institutional emergency lines are displayed at the venue helpdesk.)*

| Emergency Type | Immediate Action | Primary Escalation Point | On-Site Resource |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Medical Emergency / Illness** | Administer basic first aid; dispatch institute ambulance to BIT Mesra Dispensary | BIT Mesra Institute Dispensary / Ambulance | Stocked First-Aid Kit at Helpdesk |
| **Electrical / Fire Hazard** | Cut main zone breaker; use $\text{CO}_2$ fire extinguisher; initiate orderly evacuation | Campus Electrical Maintenance & Fire Safety | Verified Fire Extinguishers & Unblocked Exits |
| **Security / Unauthorized Entry** | Verify lanyard ID badge; escort unauthorized individuals out via Campus Security | BIT Mesra Chief Security Officer / Guard Post | Mandatory ID Badge Policy |
| **Overnight Participant Welfare** | Dedicated gender-inclusive rest zones, female & male volunteer marshals on duty | Faculty Advisor & Hostel Wardens | Separate Rest Areas & Escort Protocol |

### 4.2 Venue Safety Checklist (Pre-Event Sign-Off)
- [ ] All emergency exit doors unlocked and corridors clear of cables/furniture.
- [ ] Extension cords taped down with hazard tape across walking paths to prevent tripping.
- [ ] No daisy-chaining of high-load power strips; electrical load verified by campus electrician.
- [ ] Fully stocked First-Aid kit (bandages, antiseptic, ORS, paracetamol, basic emergency supplies) placed at the Registration/Helpdesk.
- [ ] BIT Mesra Dispensary and Campus Security notified in writing of exact event hours and expected headcount.

## 5. Decisions Made and Why
- **Decision:** Enforce mandatory lanyard ID badges for all participants, volunteers, and organizers, plus dedicated male and female volunteer marshals during overnight hours.
- **Rationale:** Guarantees strict access control and participant safety throughout the competition.
