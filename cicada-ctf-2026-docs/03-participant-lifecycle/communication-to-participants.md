## 4. Detailed Description
### 4.1 Official Communication Channels
1. **Official Email Broadcasts:** Used for formal confirmations, platform credentials, rulebooks, shortlist notifications, and certificates.
2. **Official Discord Server (`TBD Invite Link`):**
   - `#announcements` — Read-only official competition updates, wave releases, and blood alerts.
   - `#rules-and-faq` — Competition rules, flag format (`cicada{...}` or `TBD`), and platform FAQ.
   - `#support-tickets` — Automated ticket bot (`TBD: e.g., Ticket Tool`) for private participant-to-admin challenge queries (preventing public spoilers).
   - `#general` & `#find-a-team` — Community interaction and team formation.

## 5. Decisions Made and Why
- **Decision:** Require all challenge-specific support requests to go through private Discord support tickets rather than public chat channels.
- **Rationale:** Prevents accidental flag or hint leakage in public channels and maintains an auditable log of admin responses.
