## 4. Detailed Description
### 4.1 Technical Opening Execution
- Verification that all registered teams can authenticate on the scoreboard (`CTFd / Platform`).
- Verification of sanity-check challenge (`Welcome / Sanity Flag`) to confirm flag submission pipeline.
- Live countdown to `T-0` and simultaneous unlocking of Wave 1 challenges.

### 4.2 Competition Closing & Verification Window
- Automated lock of flag submissions at `T-End`.
- Collection and verification of write-ups/exploit scripts from top-ranking teams by the Development Team.
- Unfreezing of the final verified leaderboard during the Closing Ceremony.
- Closing address, recognition of organizing teams and volunteers, and official Vote of Thanks.
