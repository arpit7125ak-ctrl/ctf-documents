## 4. Detailed Description
### 4.1 Genesis of Cicada CTF 2026
 Inspired by legendary cryptographic and problem-solving puzzles and the growing demand for practical cybersecurity training, the IEEE Student Branch of BIT Mesra initiated **Cicada CTF 2026** as a flagship winter cybersecurity challenge scheduled for early December 2026.

### 4.2 Conceptual Pillars
1. **Accessibility with Depth:** Designing a progressive difficulty curve—from beginner-friendly introductory challenges to advanced multi-stage exploitation problems.
2. **Real-World Relevance:** Aligning challenge scenarios with modern vulnerability classes (OWASP Top 10, modern cryptographic flaws, cloud/container security, binary hardening bypasses).
3. **Academic & Industry Synergy:** Combining university support from BIT Mesra with industry sponsorship and mentorship.
